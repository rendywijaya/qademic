What this business is

Living explainer

Modine spent a century making radiators for trucks and cars. Today the fastest-growing slice is data-center cooling — the liquid-cooling and air-handling systems that keep racks of GPUs from melting.

How it makes money: sells engineered thermal systems; data-center segment carries higher margins (~25% gross) than legacy auto, and is growing 3–4× faster — quietly re-rating the whole company.

Where it sits: downstream of the GPU buildout — when hyperscalers order more NVDA racks, they need more cooling capacity, and VRT can't supply all of it.

Multibagger confidence — what's inside the 78

Composite
Composite is illustrative weighting of the factors above. High exposure + low priced-in is doing most of the work.
Bull case

Data-center backlog up 60% YoY; liquid cooling becomes mandatory as rack density rises. Margin mix shift re-rates the multiple.

Bear case

Legacy auto (60% of revenue) is cyclical and low-margin. A capex air-pocket at hyperscalers hits the growth segment first.

What breaks the thesis

Hyperscalers standardize on in-house or VRT cooling; or AI capex growth rolls over before the mix shift completes. Watch order book + VRT commentary.
